LED Spotlight

The LED Spotlight is a video series where every Friday we interview influential figures to LED. We record and post these spotlights with the intent of giving a wider audience a better understanding of how our club works. The people we interview come from all different groups of people: leads, mentors, or someone who has just been doing a lot of work recently. Overall, we hope to give our members a platform where they can tell people about the work they are doing.


- Elijah Mather, Media Crew of LED Robotics

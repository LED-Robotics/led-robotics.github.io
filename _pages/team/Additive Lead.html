ABOUT ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING

Additive manufacturing is a type of manufacturing technique that works by adding material to a surface, i.e, 3d printing, resin printing. We use 3d printing for custom plastic parts that can't be made by subtractive manufacturing. Additive manufacturing in club is usually used for small parts, prototypes, or objects that dont need as much strength as metal.

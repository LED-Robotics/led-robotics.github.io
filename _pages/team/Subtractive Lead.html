ABOUT SUBTRACTIVE MANUFACTURING

Subtractive manufacturing is a type of manufacturing technique that works by removing material to an object, i.e, laser cutting, CNC. We use subtractive manufacturing for custom wooden or metal parts that can't be made by additive manufacturing. Subtractive manufacturing in club is usually used for large parts, final pieces, or objects that need a lot of strength.
